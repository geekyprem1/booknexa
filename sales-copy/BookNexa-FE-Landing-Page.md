**Conclusion (centered):**
Identical slots, identical marketplace, identical 50 buyers.
He made more than four times as much. And with 24 listings against her 10, he gets found more often too, so next month the gap grows.
Talent had nothing to do with it. He made two decisions she didn't: research before each book, and a shelf around it.

> Layout: Off-white band. Photo left (skeptical person), text right.

**Headline:** "What If **Nobody Finds** My First Book?"

Good question. The answer surprises most people.

Each book is its own listing: its own cover in the results, its own seven keyword slots, its own categories. Ten books means ten different searches you can show up in. One book means one.

And the listings feed each other. Someone who lands on Volume 4 can reach the rest from your author page, and Amazon's "also bought" rows can put your other volumes in front of them. More listings, more ways in, all leading to the same shelf.

You also get to pick where those ways in are. Profit Radar™ scores demand and competition before you spend a slot, so you're not building on a street nobody walks down.

So a shelf wins twice: more buyers find you, and each one is worth more.

### 07 · So Why Isn't Everyone Building Shelves?

> Layout: Black band. Text left, photo right, then a white rounded card with the cost of mistakes.

**Headline:** So Why Isn't Everyone **Building Shelves?**

Because doing it by hand is exhausting. Ten books means ten chances to get something wrong:

- A niche with real demand and competition you can actually beat
- Puzzles that work: one solution per sudoku, a correct answer key for every page
- Margins that stay clear of the binding, with a gutter that grows as the page count grows
- A cover sized to the exact spine width for that page count and paper
- A title, subtitle, seven keywords, categories and a description, fresh for every book
- Brand-new puzzles for every volume, so you're not uploading duplicates

**White card:**
One slip (a gutter a hair too thin, a spine off by a few hundredths of an inch, a sudoku with two solutions) and you're looking at a rejection email or a one-star review.

So most people stop after one book. The second never gets started.

Paying someone else isn't cheaper: a formatter, a cover designer and a pile of tool subscriptions, all before your first royalty. And with only two slots a week, every mistake now costs you a week.

### 19 · The Royalty Maths Most Publishers Never Run

> Layout: Light band. Intro line, price table, bold one-liner, shelf table, closing, CTA.

**Headline:** The Royalty Maths **Most Publishers Never Run**

Since June 2025, Amazon.com pays a 60% royalty on paperbacks listed at $9.99 or more, and 50% on anything at $9.98 or less. Then it subtracts printing. For an 8.5×11 black-ink book up to 110 pages, printing costs $2.84.

| List price | Royalty rate | You earn per sale |
|---|---|---|
| $7.99 | 50% | ≈ $1.16 |
| $8.99 | 50% | ≈ $1.66 |
| **$9.99** | **60%** | **≈ $3.15** |
| $11.99 | 60% | ≈ $4.35 |

**One cent, from $9.98 to $9.99, is worth about a dollar on every sale.**

Now multiply by your shelf (at $9.99, about $3.15 a sale):

| Books a buyer takes from your shelf | Royalty per buyer | 100 buyers | 500 buyers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $3.15 | $315 | $1,577 |
| 2 | $6.31 | $631 | $3,154 |
| 3 | $9.46 | $946 | $4,731 |
| 4 | $12.62 | $1,262 | $6,308 |

The hard part is finding those 500 buyers, and that effort doesn't change whether one book or a whole shelf is waiting for them.

**What changes is what they're worth: $1,577 with one book, $6,308 with a four-book shelf.**

And that's before large print editions, seasonal editions and the other 13 Amazon storefronts.

*Small print: Amazon.com rates and printing cost as published by KDP. Other marketplaces use their own thresholds (for example £7.99 on Amazon.co.uk). Illustration only, not a promise of sales.*

3. **How many books do 120 credits make?**
Roughly 30 to 60 complete, listing-ready books, depending on format. A 25-puzzle word search costs 2 credits, a 30-puzzle sudoku 3, a journal or workbook 1, and each Launch Kit and Profit Radar search 1. You can add more credits from inside your account.

4. **Are the puzzles actually correct?**
Yes. Sudoku puzzles have exactly one solution, mazes come with the solved path, word searches have a highlighted answer key, and scrambles, cryptograms, connect-the-dots and math drills all include full answer keys. Flip through every PDF before you publish anyway.

5. **Can I make large print editions?**
Yes. Word search and sudoku both have a Large Print switch, so you can publish a separate edition for seniors and anyone who wants bigger type.

6. **Is a cover included?**
Yes. Every print book comes with a wraparound cover PDF (front, spine and back), sized to its exact page count, with your title, subtitle, pen name and description. It's a clean typographic design. Illustrated covers are available in Cover Studio, an optional upgrade.

7. **Will Amazon accept these books?**
Every file is built to KDP's published print specs (trim, margins, gutter, page minimum, spine width and bleed), and our sample books pass our automated KDP spec checks. Amazon still reviews every title against its content guidelines, so preview each book in KDP's previewer before you publish and answer KDP's AI-content question honestly. Your Publishing Profile keeps your AI disclosure on file. No tool can guarantee approval.

8. **Can I publish under a pen name?**
Yes. Set it in your Publishing Profile and it goes on every book and Launch Kit. Change it whenever you like.

9. **Do I keep the rights and royalties?**
Yes. The commercial license lets you publish and sell everything you create, and the royalties Amazon pays are entirely yours.

10. **Is there a monthly fee?**
No. One payment of $17 at launch. Optional upgrades are available inside, but you never have to buy one.

11. **How fast can I publish my first book?**
Most formats build in minutes, so you can upload to KDP the same day. KDP says new titles can take up to 72 hours to go live.

12. **Can I change things before I publish?**
Yes. Pick the theme, title, difficulty, puzzle or page count, trim size for journals, and large print. Generate a fresh version any time, and edit any Launch Kit text before you paste it into KDP.

13. **Where do the niche scores come from?**
Profit Radar™ uses AI to estimate demand, competition, evergreen appeal and monetization for each idea, so you can compare options fast. They're estimates, not live Amazon sales data. Check your top picks on Amazon before you build.

14. **Do I need a website, a mailing list or social media?**
No. Buyers find you through Amazon search; a pen name is all the presence you need.

15. **Does BookNexa upload to KDP for me?**
No. You stay in control of your own KDP account and upload the files yourself. The publish checklist in every Launch Kit walks you through each field.

16. **How many books can I publish per week?**
That's Amazon's call, not ours. Since 21 September 2026, KDP lets each account create 2 new titles per format per week, and the count resets every Sunday at 00:00 UTC. BookNexa lets you build up to 10 books a day and 100 a month (credits apply), so you can build ahead and fill every slot.

17. **What if a buyer returns a book?**
Returns are part of selling on Amazon. You keep them rare with puzzles that work, the right difficulty, clean print and an honest listing, which is what BookNexa is built for. Preview every book before it goes live.

18. **What if it's not for me?**
Email us within 30 days for a full refund. That's the guarantee.
